Why a sundial

Our mark is a lowercase i casting its shadow.

One of the oldest known sundials was found in Egypt's Valley of the Kings: a limestone piece from the 13th century BC with a semicircle divided into twelve sections and a peg that cast the shadow. Our identity borrows that geometry. The i in our name is the peg, the dot is the sun, and its shadow and stem together read as the hands of a clock.

It is a reminder of what we promise: software delivered on time, and kept running long after.
